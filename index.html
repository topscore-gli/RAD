The method

Clinic skills, not just flashcards

Every module is built the way radiographers actually work: protocol, position, expose, critique, decide what happens next.

🩻

Image critique rooms

Judge collimation, rotation, inspiration, exposure index, and pathology clues on real-style films — then compare with expert notes.

📐

Positioning labs

Step-by-step projections with centering points, IR placement, breathing instructions, and common errors that ruin a study.

⚛️

Radiation physics that sticks

kVp, mAs, grids, AEC, inverse square, attenuation, and digital processing — drilled with numbers you will actually use.

🛡️

Protection & ethics

ALARA, shielding, pregnancy protocols, justification, and professional communication in high-pressure rooms.

🧠

Case pathways

Follow a patient from request form to report-ready images. Learn when to adapt technique and when to stop and call.

⏱️

Board-style mocks

Timed papers that mirror national and international radiography exams, with explanations written by working radiographers.
