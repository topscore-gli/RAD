Medical Instruments

🩺 Medical Instruments & Equipment

Complete Guide with Parts • Uses • Maintenance

A

1. Adhesive Bandage

🔧 Parts
  • Absorbent pad
  • Adhesive sheet
  • Base layer
✅ Uses
  • For covering small wounds
  • To minimize contact with other surfaces
  • To fasten the healing process
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Ensure wound is clean before applying bandage
  • Change the bandage if it becomes wet or soiled
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight

2. Air Cushion

🔧 Parts
  • Air cells (inflation chambers)
  • Connecting tubes
  • Air pump/compressor unit
  • Mattress cover
✅ Uses
  • To reduce bed sores in bedridden patients
  • To distribute body weight evenly
  • To improve blood circulation in immobile patients
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Check for leaks and damages regularly
  • Clean the surface with a mild disinfectant
  • Ensure the pump filter is clean and free of dust

3. Anglepoise Lamp

🔧 Parts
  • Base
  • Articulated arms (joints/springs)
  • Lamp shade
  • Bulb
  • Power cord/switch
✅ Uses
  • Can be angled as a source of light in procedures like HSG
  • For focused illumination during minor examinations
  • As a reading light for patients
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Keep in a cool dry place
  • Wipe the shade and base with a damp cloth
  • Check the power cord for fraying or damage

4. Autoclave

🔧 Parts
  • Pressure regulating device
  • Pressure gauge
  • Safety valve
  • Autoclave lid & handles
  • Autoclave body
  • Steam release valve
  • Vacuum release valve
  • Outer stand
✅ Uses
  • For sterilization of surgical instruments
  • For chemical reactions requiring high temperature and pressure
  • To destroy microorganisms (bacteria, viruses, fungi, spores)
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Instruments that can't withstand high temperature should not be put in
  • Regular calibration for optimal accuracy
  • Switch off when required temperature is reached

5. Automatic Ventilator

🔧 Parts
  • Monitor screen
  • Control panel
  • Breathing circuit tubing
  • Humidifier
  • Flow sensors
  • Wheeled stand
✅ Uses
  • Automatically delivers oxygen to a patient
  • To assist or replace spontaneous breathing
  • To maintain adequate oxygenation and ventilation in critical care
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Periodic checks for repairs
  • Replace breathing circuits and filters regularly
  • Clean and disinfect the external surfaces and sensors
B

6. Big Valve Mask (Bag-Valve-Mask)

🔧 Parts
  • Self-inflating bag
  • Oxygen reservoir
  • Oxygen connector tubing
  • Inlet & outlet ports
  • Face mask & mask cushion
  • Pressure gauge
✅ Uses
  • To resuscitate a patient
  • To provide positive pressure ventilation
  • To deliver oxygen to a patient who is not breathing adequately
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Store in a cool dry place
  • Clean and disinfect the mask and bag after each use
  • Check valves and reservoir bag for tears or leaks
C

7. Cannula

🔧 Parts
  • Flashback chamber
  • Needle grip
  • Injection port cap
  • Luer connector & lock plug
  • Bushing & valve
  • Catheter hub + wings
  • Catheter & needle
✅ Uses
  • To drain fluid from the body
  • To administer medications
  • To provide oxygen
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Regular sterilization
  • Replacement after use
  • Flush with saline to maintain patency if left in situ

8. Cervical Osmotic Dilators

🔧 Parts
  • Handle
  • Osmotic dilator rod (laminaria or synthetic)
✅ Uses
  • Used in patients with cervical synechiae
  • To gradually dilate the cervix before procedures
  • To soften and prepare the cervix for induction
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Dispose after use
  • Store in a sterile, dry package
  • Check expiry date before use

9. Cilia Tweezers

🔧 Parts
  • Fine tips
  • Hinge mechanism
  • Handle/body
✅ Uses
  • To pick hair and tiny objects during ophthalmologic procedures
  • For removing foreign bodies from the eye
  • For precise handling of delicate tissues
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Regular cleaning and sterilization
  • Lubrication of the hinge mechanism for smooth movement
  • Inspect tips for misalignment or damage

10. Colour Blind Test / Ishihara Chart

🔧 Parts
  • Test plates (pages with colored dot patterns)
  • Instruction booklet
  • Binding
✅ Uses
  • To test patient’s ability to identify and differentiate between colours
  • To screen for red-green color blindness
  • To assess visual acuity related to color perception
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Store in a cool dry place to ensure longevity of the chart
  • Keep pages clean and free from fingerprints
  • Avoid exposure to direct sunlight to prevent fading

11. Colostomy Bag

🔧 Parts
  • Adhesive flange/wafer
  • Pouch/bag
  • Filter
  • Closure clamp/tail
✅ Uses
  • To collect waste directly from the intestine
  • To protect the stoma and surrounding skin
  • To manage fecal output in patients with a stoma
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Regular emptying and cleaning of the bag
  • Regular changing of the bag
  • Clean the stoma and surrounding skin gently with warm water
D

12. Defibrillator

🔧 Parts
  • Monitor screen
  • Control panel (energy select, charge, shock buttons)
  • Paddle handles & electrodes
  • Connecting cables
✅ Uses
  • To excite the muscles of the heart and create a heartbeat
  • To regulate abnormal heart rate
  • To terminate life-threatening cardiac arrhythmias (e.g. ventricular fibrillation)
🛠️ Maintenance
  • Keep in a cool dry place
  • Regularly check battery charge and replace as needed
  • Inspect paddles and cables for damage; clean after use
E

✅ Uses

🛠️ Maintenance
  • Regularly clean after use
  • Regularly check for wear or damage
  • Sterilize if used in sterile procedures
